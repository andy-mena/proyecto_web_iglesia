## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a bold, modern, and high-impact digital presence inspired by the energetic visual communication of contemporary ministries. It synthesizes geometric typography, immersive dark photographic sections, dynamic marquee banners, and structured content cards into an experience that feels welcoming, reverent, and forward-looking.

The system addresses both seekers looking for a welcoming home and active congregants seeking discipleship, event details, and service streams. It moves away from dated traditional church aesthetics, adopting an impactful editorial stance: massive uppercase display headlines, rich photographic overlays, and intentional bursts of crimson and gold against deep slate navy foundations.

The design movement bridges **Corporate / Modern** clarity with **High-Contrast / Bold** visual hierarchy. Crisp off-white backgrounds keep administrative and schedule-oriented content effortless to scan, while dramatic deep-navy surfaces create cinematic theater for worship livestreams and primary call-to-action blocks.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate tension between the deep, reverent weight of slate navy and the urgent vitality of rich crimson, tempered by warm biblical gold accents.

- **Primary (`#9B0000`)**: Deep crimson red reserved for primary conversion touchpoints ("Plan A Visit", "Give Online", register actions), critical indicators, and hero emphasis text.
- **Secondary (`#1F2937` / `#111827`)**: Slate navy and rich midnight tones. Used to build immersive dark hero segments, footer basements, and high-contrast section containers that frame photography.
- **Tertiary (`#C19A2D`)**: Warm, regal gold used for secondary CTA buttons ("What We Believe"), category flags, date markers, and subtle decorative rule lines.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) and warm alabaster (`#F8F9FA` / `#F3F4F6`) host readable text, event schedules, and ministry directories without visual noise. Dark typography utilizes `#111827` to preserve contrast accessibility standards (WCAG AAA for body text).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is defined by high-impact geometric structuralism paired with crisp, functional legibility. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Montserrat`)**: Rendered in heavy weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking for punchy presence. Display statements ("Love God. Love People. Change the World.") command attention with uncompromising solidity.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, tall x-height sans-serif providing optimal legibility across desktop screens and mobile church apps. Used for sermons, sermon notes, and ministry narratives.
- **Labels & Super-Headlines (`Montserrat`)**: Styled uppercase with generous letter tracking (`0.12em`) to introduce sections such as `LATEST HAPPENINGS` or `WATCH & LISTEN`.
- **Marquee/Ticker Type**: Outsized 48px to 64px display tokens deployed either as solid fills or hollow outlines (`-webkit-text-stroke: 1px`) to create rhythmic horizontal banners.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout follows a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Vertical Rhythm**: High contrast between tight internal component padding (`1rem` to `1.5rem`) and expansive vertical band separation (`4.5rem` to `6rem`). Full-bleed background bands alternate between deep navy (`#111827`), crisp white (`#FFFFFF`), and soft gray (`#F8F9FA`).
- **Grid Composition**:
  - **Hero & Message Sections**: Asymmetric split grids with left-aligned editorial stacks and right-weighted dynamic photo matrices.
  - **Ministry & Event Cards**: 3-column layouts on desktop (spanning 4 columns each), reflowing to 1 column on mobile.
  - **Ticker Banners**: Edge-to-edge overflow blocks with zero vertical guttering against adjacent image grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: Flat base background (`#FFFFFF` or `#111827`).
  - `Level 1 (Card Default)`: Solid color surface bounded by a crisp 1px border (`#E5E7EB` in light mode, `#374151` in dark mode).
  - `Level 2 (Active/Hover)`: Light ambient rise with a tight 4px blur (`0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`) paired with a micro-translation (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Image Depth**: Dark gradient scums (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0) 20%, rgba(17,24,39,0.95) 100%)`) overlaid directly atop media tiles to guarantee text contrast for inline card labels and action buttons.
- **Outlines**: Intentional 1px and 2px borders applied to outline buttons and interactive cards to maintain architectural structure.

## Shapes

The design adopts a cohesive **Soft (`1`)** roundedness setting. 

- Core interactive elements (buttons, inputs, status badges) utilize a slight `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius to soften edges while maintaining an architectural, dependable profile.
- Media cards and interactive surface cards utilize an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius (`rounded-lg`) to smoothly bound photography without feeling bubble-like or juvenile.
- Pill shapes are strictly avoided; rectangular solidity reinforces institutional longevity and conviction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid crimson background (`#9B0000`), white uppercase/semi-bold Montserrat text, `0.25rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Hover shifts to `#7D0000`.
- **Secondary Button (Gold)**: Solid warm gold background (`#C19A2D`), white text, used for high-importance secondary actions like theological statements or donations.
- **Dark Inverted Outline**: 2px solid white border, transparent background, white text with trailing right-arrow icon (`→`). Used directly over photographic and dark navy hero sections.

### Event & Ministry Cards
- **Photo Overlay Cards**: Full vertical aspect image (3:4 ratio) featuring a rich gradient overlay, bold white Montserrat title anchored to the lower quadrant, and a bottom-aligned primary button or text-link.
- **Schedule Cards**: Solid white background, 1px `#E5E7EB` border, featuring a graphic header block, event title, date metadata accompanied by calendar glyphs, and a bottom utility button.

### Ticker / Marquee Ribbons
- Full-bleed horizontal scrolling ribbon alternating between solid black/white and outlined uppercase text ("WELCOME HOME", "JOIN US THIS SUNDAY"). Serves as a dynamic visual divider between high-density content blocks.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Inset light gray background (`#F9FAFB`) with 1px border (`#D1D5DB`). On focus, shifts to a 1px border with a soft 2px ring in primary crimson (`#9B0000`). Labels sit above in uppercase `12px` Montserrat.

### Sermon & Media Players
- Dark-themed card (`#1F2937`) with embedded 16:9 thumbnail, progress scrubber styled in warm gold (`#C19A2D`), and floating play trigger badge with 4px corner radius.

## Páginas del Sitio (Estructura)

1. **Home (Inicio)** — Hero oscuro con imagen, título grande, botón "Planifica tu visita". Secciones de: bienvenida, horarios, eventos, ministerios, prédicas y CTA final.
2. **Nosotros (Sobre Nosotros)** — Hero con imagen. Historia, misión, visión, valores fundamentales y equipo pastoral.
3. **Eventos** — Hero oscuro. Calendario/lista de próximos eventos con tarjetas (fecha, título, botón "Ver detalles").
4. **Evento Detalle** — Un evento específico. Título, fecha, hora, lugar, descripción y botón de inscripción.
5. **Ministerios** — Hero. Grid de tarjetas (Niños, Jóvenes, Adultos, etc.) con botón "Conocer más".
6. **Ministerios** — Hero oscuro con etiqueta "Comunidad y Servicio". Tarjeta de introducción superpuesta. Grid de 3 tarjetas (Niños, Jóvenes, Adultos) con etiqueta de edad en rojo, lista de características con check y horario al pie
7. **Prédicas** — Hero oscuro. Lista de sermones con reproductor de video/audio.
8. **Contacto** — Hero oscuro. Formulario de contacto, información de la sede y mapa.
9. **Soy Nuevo** — Hero oscuro. Qué esperar, horarios y mapa.
